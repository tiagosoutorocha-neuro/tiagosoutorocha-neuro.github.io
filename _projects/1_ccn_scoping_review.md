#### Overview

Computational cognitive neuroscience (CCN) brings together cognitive science, neuroscience and computational modeling to explain how the brain gives rise to cognition. The field changed quickly over the last decade, driven by deep learning, large neural datasets and new modeling frameworks.

This scoping review maps **ten years of CCN research (2015–2025)**. It covers the main methodological advances, the paradigm shifts that reshaped the field, and the frontiers still open.

#### Approach

- A systematic search and screening of the literature, following scoping review guidelines.
- Charting of the included studies by topic, methods and type of contribution.
- A synthesis of trends, paradigm shifts and open questions for the field.
