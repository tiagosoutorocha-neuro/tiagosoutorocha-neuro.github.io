**NeuroSeizure-EEG** received its computer program registration from the **Brazilian National Institute of Industrial Property (INPI)**. The software analyzes the pre-ictal period of the electroencephalogram (EEG), the window that precedes a seizure, to support epileptic seizure prediction.

#### Registration details

- **Full title:** NeuroSeizure-EEG: Software for Pre-Ictal Period Analysis for the Prediction of Epileptic Seizures through Electroencephalography
- **Registration (process) number:** BR512026008531-4
- **Holder:** State University of Paraíba (UEPB)
- **Authors:** Souto, S. F., Dantas, K. V. O., Figueiredo Neto, J. A., Paixão, L. M., Valério, D. P. S., **Rocha, T. S.**
- **Programming language:** Python
- **Creation and publication date:** August 18, 2026
- **Certificate issued:** October 6, 2026
- **Validity:** 50 years, under Brazilian Law 9,609/1998

The registration formally protects the software's authorship and makes UEPB the rights holder.
