On August 24 we started data collection for **Cidade Madura**, an extension project that brings cognitive monitoring to older adults in community settings. It combines standardized cognitive assessment, accessible neurotechnology and health-promotion activities.

**What the project does**

- Follows **at least 30 older adults** through **two cycles** of cognitive assessment and monitoring with a **wearable EEG**.
- Runs **at least six educational and cognitive-stimulation activities**, such as workshops, conversation circles and cognitive-stimulation sessions, with about **60 participants** (older adults, family members and the community).
- Focuses on healthy aging and on strengthening self-care.

**Expected outcomes:** identifying early signs of cognitive decline, encouraging continued participation in the activities, and building a data repository for longitudinal follow-up. The project also aims to improve care, widen access to technology and strengthen ties between the university and the community.
