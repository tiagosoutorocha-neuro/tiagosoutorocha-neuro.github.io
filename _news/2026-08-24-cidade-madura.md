On August 24, 2026 we started data collection for **Cidade Madura**, an extension project that brings cognitive monitoring to the older adults who live in the **Cidade Madura condominium**. The project combines standardized cognitive assessment, accessible neurotechnology and health-promotion activities, and it strengthens the connection between the university and the community.

#### Goals

- Follow **at least 30 older adults** through **two cycles** of cognitive assessment and monitoring with a **wearable EEG**.
- Run **at least six educational and cognitive-stimulation activities**, such as workshops, conversation circles and cognitive-stimulation sessions, with about **60 participants** (older adults, family members and the community).
- Promote healthy aging and self-care.

#### How data collection works

Each participant goes through three stages. Splitting the stages this way reduces the bias of having several different examiners.

1. **EEG recording**, with simultaneous **heart rate variability (HRV)** recording (about 20 min)
2. **Sociodemographic questionnaire and MoCA** cognitive screening (about 40 min)
3. **Anamnesis and DASS-21** (about 45 min). The anamnesis helps us understand each person's context, self-perception and the impact on daily life.

Sessions happen every two weeks, with a full three-stage assessment of three older adults per collection day. A physiotherapy PhD student (UFRN) and a psychology professional supervise the sessions.

#### Contributions to the older adults and the Cidade Madura condominium

- **An individual report for each person.** Every participant receives a personal report in plain language with an **individual analysis** of their own results: cognitive screening, emotional symptoms, and brain (EEG) and heart (HRV) activity.
- **Referrals based on the integrated analysis.** We interpret the cognitive, emotional, EEG and HRV results together. When that integrated picture points to a need, the participant receives **referrals** to the appropriate follow-up.
- **Follow-up over time.** The two assessment cycles let each person follow their changes over time, and help detect early signs of decline while there is still room to act.
- **Benefits for the whole condominium.** The Cidade Madura community gets regular cognitive monitoring, educational and cognitive-stimulation activities open to residents and families, and access to neurotechnology that is rarely available outside research centers.

#### Expected outcomes

- Identify early signs of cognitive decline.
- Encourage continued participation in the activities.
- Build a data repository for longitudinal follow-up.

The project also aims to improve care and widen access to neurotechnology for older adults.
