At the **17th Courses and Conferences on Neuroscience and Behavior (CCNEC)**, held September 15–17, 2026, our group won **first and second place for Best Work** among 80 competing researchers.

- **1st place:** _Individualizing the Pre-Ictal Window for Machine Learning-Based Epileptic Seizure Prediction: A Study on Clinical and Wearable-Device EEG Data_. Valério, D. P. S., Souto, S. F., Paixão, L. M., Figueiredo, J. A., **Rocha, T. S.** (co-author)
- **2nd place:** _Explainable Machine Learning for Alzheimer's Disease Detection via Resting-State Electroencephalography_. **Rocha, T. S.** (first author), Gouveia, G. S. M., Oliveira, S. I. A., Brito, A. M., Souto, S. F.

The NeuroComp group presented **six works** at the event. See them all on the [publications page]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}).
