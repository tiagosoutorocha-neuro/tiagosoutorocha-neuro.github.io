Our article **"Understanding Advances in Computational Cognitive Neuroscience Over the Past Ten Years: A Scoping Review"** was accepted for publication in **_Cognitive Computation_** (Springer Nature) on September 8, 2026.

The review maps a decade of computational cognitive neuroscience, summarizing its main advances, paradigm shifts and open frontiers. The article is now in press. Until the final version is published, the preprint is available on [PsyArXiv](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/rdeub_v1).

**Authors:** **Rocha, T. S.**, Brito, A. M., Lins, M. V. S., Barbosa, T. P., Alves, J. P. P., Maciel, E. Q., Paixão, L. M., Souto, S. F.
