Scoping review accepted in Cognitive Computation

September 08, 2026

Our article “Understanding Advances in Computational Cognitive Neuroscience Over the Past Ten Years: A Scoping Review” was accepted for publication in Cognitive Computation (Springer Nature) on September 8, 2026.

The review maps a decade of computational cognitive neuroscience (CCN). It covers the main methodological advances, the paradigm shifts that reshaped the field, and the open frontiers for the coming years. The work grew out of the scoping review we started in March 2025, whose first results we presented at CCNEC 2025.

The article is now in press. Until the final version comes out, the preprint is available on PsyArXiv.

Authors: Rocha, T. S., Brito, A. M., Lins, M. V. S., Barbosa, T. P., Alves, J. P. P., Maciel, E. Q., Paixão, L. M., Souto, S. F.
