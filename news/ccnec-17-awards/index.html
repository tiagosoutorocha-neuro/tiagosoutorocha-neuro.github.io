1st and 2nd place for Best Work at the 17th CCNEC

September 17, 2026

At the 17th Courses and Conferences on Neuroscience and Behavior (CCNEC), held September 15–17, 2026, our group won first and second place for Best Work among 80 competing researchers.

Awarded works

  • 1st place. Individualizing the Pre-Ictal Window for Machine Learning-Based Epileptic Seizure Prediction: A Study on Clinical and Wearable-Device EEG Data. Valério, D. P. S., Souto, S. F., Paixão, L. M., Figueiredo, J. A., Rocha, T. S. (co-author)
  • 2nd place. Explainable Machine Learning for Alzheimer’s Disease Detection via Resting-State Electroencephalography. Rocha, T. S. (first author), Gouveia, G. S. M., Oliveira, S. I. A., Brito, A. M., Souto, S. F.

All works presented by the NeuroComp group

  1. Explainable Machine Learning for Alzheimer’s Disease Detection via Resting-State Electroencephalography (2nd place)
  2. Individualizing the Pre-Ictal Window for Machine Learning-Based Epileptic Seizure Prediction (1st place)
  3. Beyond Spectral Power: A Comparative Study of EEG Biomarker Categories Using Machine Learning for Alzheimer’s Disease Screening
  4. Sleep, Depressive Symptoms, and Cognitive Performance in Older Adults: A Possible Mediation Pathway
  5. Electroencephalography in the Detection of Alzheimer’s Disease and Mild Cognitive Impairment: An Integrative Review
  6. The Hippocampus as a Central Modulator in the Predictive Theory of Mind: An Integrative Review

The posters and author lists are on the publications page.
